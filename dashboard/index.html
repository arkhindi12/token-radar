The value model

The fit, on this snapshot

Leave-one-out, in multiples

Each formula is refit with one protocol left out, predicts that protocol, and the median miss is reported as the multiple exp(median |log error|): 1.00× is a perfect fit, 2.00× a typical error of a factor of two. The same rows are then predicted three ways that do not see them: leave one out, an alternating split, and a time split.

What the fit read, and what it dropped

Out of sample, three ways

The claim the board leans on most is that its misses are measured, not assumed. A random split can flatter a model whose data drifted; a time split cannot, because it trains on the protocols listed longest ago and tests on the ones listed most recently, which is the reader's situation.
